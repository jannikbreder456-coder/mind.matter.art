Artist Statement

Jannik Breder

I build cyber-organic visuals for people, events, spaces, and projects that need a distinct visual world. My work moves between drawing, print, installation, surface design, and identity marks.

Organic Dreamscapes is the common thread: forms that feel grown, transformed, and slightly unfamiliar. Each project is developed for its actual context, from paper and skin to rooms, bars, stages, and visual identities.
